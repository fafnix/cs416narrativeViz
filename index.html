Exploring Major Milestones in Adam's Life
Using Federal Reserve Unemployment Data

CIS 416: Data Visualization at UIUC

I earned my undergraduate degree in May of 2011. Although I graduated from a well-known university (UC Berkeley, go bears!), I struggled to find my first job.

After moving back into my parents' house, I took up part-time dog training with someone who had previously trained my dogs. It is still a story which elicits interest to this day, but it definitely was not where I expected to land.

When trying to understand why I was struggling, I was told time and again that I was living through the effects of a once-in-a-lifetime recession and the ensuing unemployment effects.

According to Wikipedia, the Great Recession was from December 2007 through June 2009, with unemployment peaking in October of 2009 at 10%.

Graduating in 2011 meant trying to enter the workplace while unemployment rates had still not returned to the pre-December 2007 rates, still at 9%. Going through these data validate some of the difficulty I felt and lived through.

Although the Great Recession was difficult, it turned out to not be a once-in-a-lifetime event. Wikipedia describes that the COVID-19 recession would become the second, large recession of my adult life.

Although I was employed for this recession, there would be two major impacts to my life. First, my fiancée and I had planned to get married in June of 2020. Although we still held a small ceremony, our larger ceremony in Half Moon Bay, California was postponed by the California lockdowns and then canceled. The partial refunds and effects on vendors were mirrored in the larger economy, with unemployment rates peaking in April of 2020 at 14.8%.

The second impact was my now wife and I would both be affected by layoffs in 2023 as well. Layoffs which the company which employed both of us tied to overhiring towards the end of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Now that you have explored some of the major milestones of my life alongside the associated US unemployment data, please feel free to explore major milestones in your life!
